Our Core Mission

CONSTITUTIONAL OBJECTIVES

Defined by Section 2 of our governing constitution to build a crime-resilient community.

01

Promote Safety & Security

Proactively foster a peaceful, protected environment for every resident and family across the Mokgoba community.

02

Neighbourhood Watch & Patrols

Establish, mobilize, and run disciplined neighborhood street watches and continuous nighttime patrols.

03

Police & CPF Collaboration

Maintain active strategic partnerships with the South African Police Service (SAPS), local CPF, and emergency responders.

04

Crime Prevention Education

Conduct workshops and alert bulletins to educate residents on high-risk tactics, home security, and self-protection.

05

Victim Support Services

Offer immediate compassionate assistance, community solidarity, and guidance to victims of violent crime and traumatic incidents.

06

Security Infrastructure Advocacy

Advocate for street lighting, physical safety barriers, and upgraded civil infrastructure to deter illicit criminal actions.

Accountability & Trust

GOVERNANCE & CODE OF CONDUCT

Integrity is paramount. Our organisation functions strictly under democratic oversight and accountability rules.

Executive Committee
  • Led by Chairperson, Deputy Chairperson, Secretary, and Treasurer.
  • Elected democratically at the Annual General Meeting (AGM).
  • Accountable to regular reporting and transparent voting.
Financial Controls
  • Dedicated registered bank account in the organisation's name.
  • Chairperson authorization threshold capped at R5,000.
  • Expenses above R5,000 require full executive committee approval.
Anti-Sabotage Clause
  • Zero tolerance for conflicts of interest or split loyalties[cite: 2].
  • Members cannot create, join, or support rival breakaway security groups.
  • Breaches trigger prompt disciplinary review and expulsion.
Fair Hearing Process
  • Strict 14 days' written notice issued before any disciplinary hearing.
  • Full right to call witnesses, submit proof, and appeal to the AGM.
  • Grievances acknowledged within 7 days by the Secretary.
